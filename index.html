壱 0 1  —  P H I L O S O P H Y
道

Three hundred years of needle and ink, rendered through the vocabulary of a future that hasn't arrived.

Kurohebi was founded on a simple, immovable conviction: that the irezumi tradition — its dragons and serpents, its cherry-blossom weather, its oni and koi — survives only when it is allowed to breathe in new air. We do not flatten the past into nostalgia. We do not gild the present into spectacle.

Every piece begins on rice paper, in the hand of a master. It ends on skin, under instruments calibrated to the tenth of a millimetre. What sits between is dialogue — between artist and wearer, between a centuries-old grammar and a body that will carry it into the century to come.

道  ·  THE WAY — Mori Kentaro, Founding Artist, 2087

参 0 3  —  T H E  P R O C E S S

Five movements from first conversation to healed skin.

  1. 壱/01

    Consultation

    90 min  ·  In studio  ·  By appointment

    A long conversation. We listen for what the piece is asking to be — the body it will live on, the life that body intends to lead. No pressure to commit; many conversations end without a needle.

  2. 弐/02

    Design

    2–6 weeks  ·  Sumi ink on washi paper

    Drawn entirely by hand. We share progress in two reviews; revisions are encouraged early and welcomed late. The final design is yours regardless of whether you proceed.

  3. 参/03

    Stencil & Placement

    2 hr  ·  Day-of, on body

    The drawing meets the skin. We map flow with the muscle, the breath, the way the piece will move when you do. Nothing begins until placement is right.

  4. 肆/04

    Session

    4–8 hr per sitting  ·  Single artist throughout

    Full pieces are completed across multiple sittings, weeks apart. The same hand begins and ends each work. Tea between hours; quiet, when quiet is what's needed.

  5. 伍/05

    Aftercare

    14 days critical  ·  Lifetime touch-ups

    Detailed care kit and protocol provided. Every Kurohebi piece carries a lifetime warranty against fade and line-loss; touch-ups are by appointment, complimentary.
